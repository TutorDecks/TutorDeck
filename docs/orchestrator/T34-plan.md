# T34 plan: bank statement matching for centres

Branch `claude/t34-plan`. Plan only: no product code, no SQL, nothing deployed, LIVE and DEV untouched. Written 2026-11-26. Owner approved the feature the same day. Customer: **the first managed centre** (no names or terms here). Read: CLAUDE.md, `assets/js/org-settings.js` (section "How parents pay", l.103-120), `assets/js/money.js`, `docs/orchestrator/T4-plan.md` (branch `claude/t4-plan`), and `db/migration_centre_money.sql` on branch `claude/t4-s1-tables` (tables, guards, audit redaction). Anything I could not confirm says **not verified**. Retention and PDPA points are for the owner's lawyer; this is not advice.

## Summary
- **What it is:** an admin uploads a bank statement CSV; TutorDeck shows the lines and matches incoming money to open invoices by rules. Exact matches are marked paid and logged; everything else goes to a **review queue** where a person confirms, picks another invoice, splits, marks a deposit, or marks "not ours". Nothing is ever edited: a wrong match is **reversed** (a reversal row, as for any payment).
- **Today:** only a stub exists: `payments.modes` has `statement_matching` in `soonOptions` and `payments.statement_bank` (DBS / OCBC / UOB / custom) is `soon`. Nothing reads them. There are no payment tables on `dev` yet; they come from T4 S1-S3.
- **Bank formats are not verified.** I have no sample export and could not read any bank's own documentation here. So the **primary mechanism is a column-mapping step, saved per organisation**; DBS, OCBC and UOB are only **presets to confirm against real sample files** before they are switched on (section 2).
- **Parsing in the browser, matching and writing in the database.** The file never leaves the browser; only the mapped fields go up, with account numbers cut to the last 4 digits. The rules live in one place, SQL, with tests.
- **Money path = T4's.** A confirmed match calls the same internal function as manual payment recording (T4 S3), so allocations, partial payments, overpayments and reversals behave identically and there is one audit trail.
- **Duplicates:** every line gets a fingerprint; the same file twice, or two files with overlapping dates, add nothing new.
- **Retention (owner's rule):** raw lines are deleted once resolved; only a match record (date, amount, link to the payment, rule, who confirmed) and a name-free fingerprint stay.
- **Seven small steps** (section 8), each clickable on DEV with fake statements. First release needs T4 S1-S3; deposits and refunds need S4-S5. AI help is a later, optional step.
- Six decisions for the owner in section 9. **Biggest blocker: a sample export from the first managed centre's bank, names removed.**

## 1. The flow
1. **Upload** (Payments > Statements). The admin picks the file, an **account label** ("Main account") and a **mapping** (saved, or a preset once confirmed, or "new").
2. **Parse in the browser** (why: the mapping screen needs an instant preview of the first rows; the full file, with names and account numbers, never has to leave the device; no server function, encoding or upload-size problems). The parser handles BOM, quotes, CRLF, thousands separators, header rows above the table, a totals row below it. Amounts are read **from the text to whole cents** with the money-module text parser (never `parseFloat * 100`, `money.js` l.25).
3. **Preview:** N lines, credits total, debits total, date range, and a warning if the range overlaps an earlier batch ("14 of these lines were imported before and will be skipped").
4. **Import:** the browser sends normalised lines (date, signed cents, reference text, payer text, bank's own reference if mapped) to one function; the database stores them, drops duplicates, and **runs the rules**.
5. **Rules, strongest first** (credit lines only; open = issued, not void, balance > 0). The date window (default: from 7 days before the issue date to 90 days after the due date, a setting) applies to all:
   - **A. Invoice number found in the reference** (the centre's own `number_text`, case and spacing ignored) **and the amount equals that invoice's balance** → exact, auto-confirmed.
   - **B. Known payer** (a remembered payer-to-household link, section 3) **and exactly one open invoice of that household has exactly this balance** → exact, auto-confirmed.
   - **C. Amount only** (exactly one open invoice in the whole centre has this balance, no number, no known payer) → **suggestion only**, never auto: the same amount recurs too often.
   - **D. Household's whole open balance** (the line equals the sum of all, or exactly one subset, of a household's open invoices; at most 10 open invoices tried) → suggestion "pays 3 invoices", one click to confirm.
   - Anything else, or two candidates, or a mismatch, or a number found with the wrong amount → **review queue**, with the reason in plain words ("INV-12 found, but S$20.00 short").
6. **Auto-confirm is a centre choice** (`payments.statement.auto_confirm`: `exact_only` default / `never`, product principle in CLAUDE.md). With `never` every line waits for a person.
7. **Review actions:** *Confirm*; *Choose other invoice*; *Split* across invoices (several allocations on one payment); *Mark as deposit*; *Mark as refund out*; *Not ours*. Confirming a household link from a name the app has not seen offers "Remember this payer for next time" (ticked by default).
8. **Undo:** every matched, ignored or deposit line has *Undo*, which reverses the payment (T4's reversal row, negative allocations) and returns the line to the queue with the reason typed.

## 2. Bank formats: what is known, what is not
I could not open a real export or a bank's own documentation from here. **Not verified, for DBS, OCBC and UOB alike:** exact column names and order; whether a file has header rows above the table or a totals row below; the date format (day/month/year text vs ISO) and its language; whether debit and credit are two columns, one signed column, or amount plus a Dr/Cr marker; whether amounts carry thousands separators or brackets for negatives; the text encoding and delimiter; whether the PayNow remark and the payer's name are separate fields, one description field, or sometimes missing (some channels may not show the payer name at all, which would switch off rule B for that centre); whether personal and business online banking export differently; the maximum length of the reference the payer can type. I will not guess names. What is safe to assume in general: the export is a delimited text file with one transaction per row and a date, an amount and some description text.

**Therefore the design:**
- **Mapping is the primary mechanism.** Per organisation, saved by name: delimiter, number of header rows to skip, footer rule (skip rows with an empty date), date column and **date format picked from a list and shown with a live example**, **amount mode** (one signed column / separate debit and credit columns / amount plus Dr-Cr column), reference columns (one or several, joined), payer column (optional), bank-reference column (optional), plus the sign convention. The preview shows five parsed rows beside the raw ones; a mapping cannot be saved until every previewed date and amount parses.
- **The three banks are presets** (`payments.statement_bank`): a preset only pre-fills a mapping and is switched on **only after it passes a real sample file**. Until then the wizard keeps "Custom CSV columns" as the working choice and the presets stay `soon`.
- **What a sample from the customer must confirm** (names removed, about 30 lines, from the same login and channel the centre will use): the column headers and order; header and footer rows; date and amount formats; where an incoming PayNow's remark and payer name land; what a debit looks like (refund, tutor payment, fee); a line with a long or multi-line reference; a same-day pair of identical amounts; an export covering a month end. I will ask for it before step M3.

## 3. Memory, duplicates, partials, deposits, refunds
- **Payer links:** when a person confirms a match, the normalised payer text is stored as a link to the household (`times_used`, `last_used`). Next time rule B uses it. Admins can see and **forget** links (Statements > Payers). A link never overrides a mismatch in amount.
- **Duplicate protection:** fingerprint = SHA-256 (built into Postgres 11+; **not verified** that the DEV project is on 11+, but Supabase projects are) of `org + account label + date + signed cents + normalised reference + bank reference`, **plus an occurrence number**: the nth identical line in a file gets n. So two real S$200 payments on the same day with the same remark are both kept, while the same file twice, or a file overlapping an earlier one, only adds lines whose (fingerprint, n) is new. Fingerprints are kept after the raw lines are deleted and hold no readable name.
- **Partial payments:** a credit smaller than the balance is allocated only if `payments.partial_payments` is on (confirmed by a person, never automatic); if off it stays in review marked "short by S$X".
- **Overpayments:** per `payments.overpayments`: `credit` records the excess as household credit; `refund` leaves the excess flagged for a manual refund. Never into a deposit silently (T4 rule).
- **Deposits** (needs T4 S4): a deposit invoice is an ordinary open invoice, so rules A-D find it by number. **Mark as deposit** on a stray credit allocates it to the chosen student's open deposit invoice; with none open the line waits ("no deposit invoice yet").
- **Refunds out** (needs T4 S5): debit lines are **never auto-matched**. The review screen offers refunds in state `approved` with the same amount; confirming calls S5's mark-paid step (date, bank reference as the reference, no account number). Other debits (rent, tutor pay) → *Not ours*.
- **Payment before invoice:** no open invoice → stays in review, "no open invoice yet", with *Record as household credit* offered only if the centre keeps overpayments as credit (how T4 S3 treats credit with no allocation: **not verified, S3 is not built**).
- **Reversal:** `stmt_reverse_match` calls the payment reversal, marks the match reversed and puts a **stub line** (date, amount, no text) back in the queue so it can be rematched without the deleted names.

## 4. Data model
New tables, all with `org_id`, same-org composite keys, **no insert/update/delete policy**, read policy by `org_can(org,'record_payments')`, writes only through `SECURITY DEFINER` functions (names proposed). Tutors, parents, solo orgs and other centres: `NOT_FOUND`. Any table with `org_id` and `on delete cascade` is already covered by account deletion (CLAUDE.md).
| Table | Holds |
|---|---|
| `statement_mappings` | name, preset or custom, the mapping fields of section 2, version; a new version is a new row |
| `statement_batches` | account label, date range, line counts by outcome, mapping used, imported by/at. **No file name** (names can sit in file names) |
| `statement_lines` | batch, row number, date, signed cents, reference text, payer text, bank reference, fingerprint + n, state `new / review / matched / ignored`, suggestion (invoice ids, rule, reason), `purge_after`. **Deleted when resolved** |
| `statement_fingerprints` | (org, fingerprint, n) unique, first batch, state. Kept. No text |
| `statement_matches` | line fingerprint, date, cents, kind (invoice / deposit / refund / credit / ignored), rule (`invoice_no`, `payer_link`, `manual`), `payment_id`, confirmed by (`auto` or a person), at, reversed by/at/reason. Kept. No names |
| `statement_payer_links` | org, normalised payer text, household, times used, last used. Editable (forget) |
**Functions:** `stmt_save_mapping`, `stmt_import_lines(batch, lines)` (dedupe, store, match), `stmt_review_queue`, `stmt_confirm(line, allocations)`, `stmt_mark_deposit`, `stmt_mark_refund`, `stmt_ignore`, `stmt_reverse_match`, `stmt_forget_payer`. Each checks `record_payments`, that `statement_matching` is in `payments.modes` for the organisation, and the centre's settings. Confirming calls T4's single internal payment function (payment + allocations + reversal live there, not duplicated). Payment `method` stays one of the S1 values (`bank`, or `manual_paynow` when the line is a PayNow); no new method is needed because the source is on the match record.
**Settings added** (`org-settings.js`, validated in `org_validate_settings`, enums checked by `tests/org-settings.test.js`; `soon` until built): `payments.statement.auto_confirm` (`exact_only` / `never`), `payments.statement.window_before_days` (0-30, default 7), `payments.statement.window_after_days` (0-365, default 90). Money rules follow the settings in force on the line's date; process rules as of today (CLAUDE.md).
**Audit:** same shape as `centre_money_audit` (S1 l.439): `reference`, `payer_name`, `note`, `reason`, `description` are replaced by "[redacted]" in the audit copy; this plan adds `ref_text`, `payer_text`, `payer_key`, `bank_reference`. **Account numbers:** the browser keeps the last 4 digits of any column mapped as an account; the database also masks any run of 9+ digits in reference text to its last 4 (invoice numbers are short, so they survive; a test proves it).
**Retention:** a line is deleted the moment it is resolved. Unresolved lines are deleted after **60 days** (proposal; decision 3). Fingerprints and match records stay as the centre's payment records, as for any payment. The purge runs inside the import and confirm functions (no cron needed).

## 5. Tests
- **Synthetic generator** `tests/fixtures/statement-gen.js` (seeded, so runs repeat): FAKE households, FAKE payer names from a fixed list, UENs and accounts that are obviously invalid, writes CSVs in several layouts (signed column; debit/credit columns; amount + Dr-Cr; header rows; footer total; day-first and ISO dates; quoted commas; BOM; CRLF), each labelled "layout assumed, not a real export". **The repo is public: no real statement is ever a fixture, even anonymised.**
- **Unit** (`tests/statement-core.test.js`): parser per layout; mapping validation; text-to-cents with `1,234.50`, `(12.00)`, `S$12`, `-0.05`; fingerprint stability (case, spacing) and occurrence numbering; account masking.
- **SQL** (`tests/sql/`, run by `bash tests/sql/run.sh`): one test per rule, per value of each setting (`exact_only`, `never`; window edges at day -8, -7, +90, +91), per refusal (non-member, solo org, tutor, parent, other centre, missing `record_payments`, mode not enabled), audit redaction, no names left after resolve, purge at 60 days, reversal returns a stub, the same file twice adds zero, overlapping files add only new lines.
- **Edge cases:** rounding (exact cents only; S$0.01 off goes to review showing the difference; no tolerance in v1); two invoices of the same amount in one household (number in the reference picks one; without a number both are proposed, never auto); the same amount in two households (rule C refuses, rule B needs the link); payment before invoice; a bulk payment by one parent for three siblings (rule D, one subset only, 11 open invoices skipped as too many); a number found but wrong amount; two numbers in one reference; a partial then the rest; a debit line; zero amounts; a negative credit; future-dated lines.
- **UI check** (as `tests/tutor-page.check.js`): upload a generated file, review, confirm, undo, at 1280 and 390 px.

## 6. AI-assisted matching (later, optional, not in the first release)
**Would add:** reading free-text remarks ("Jan lessons for Ethan"), tolerance for typos, nicknames and transliterated names, proposing a split from a message, ranking review-queue suggestions. **Rules:** off by default per centre (a setting, with a plain notice that payer names go to an outside processor); **only amount, date, payer text and reference text are sent**, never account numbers, other families' names or invoice contents beyond the candidate invoice numbers; proposals are stored as suggestions with source `ai`; **every proposal is confirmed by a person**, never auto; a processor agreement and the PDPA notice are the owner's and lawyer's to settle first. The first release has no network call to any AI service.

## 7. Screen (Payments > Statements, desktop first)
One primary button: **Upload statement**. Tabs: **Needs review (n)** (default when n > 0) / **Matched** / **Ignored** / **Batches** (history, never the raw lines). Review row: date, amount, bank reference text (as the bank wrote it, masked), suggested invoice(s) with the reason in one line, then *Confirm* and a menu (Choose other invoice, Split, Mark as deposit, Mark as refund out, Not ours). Matched and Ignored rows carry *Undo*. Mapping editor opens from the upload step ("Check columns"), with the preview beside it. House style (Manrope, teal only for primary/paid, owed red for shortfalls); one-time `TL.coach` tip; phones can review and confirm, upload and mapping are desktop-first. Where this lives in the T4 navigation: **not verified**, depends on T4 S3.

## 9. Risks and decisions for the owner (recommendation first)
1. **Obtain a sample export** from the first managed centre's bank: names removed (or replaced by X), ~30 lines, from the login and channel they will use, including an incoming PayNow, a debit and an awkward reference. **Recommend:** ask now; build M0-M2 with the mapping step meanwhile; no preset ships without a sample. Alternative: ship only "custom mapping" and let each centre do it once. Risk if the bank omits the payer name: rule B is unusable for them; rule A (invoice number) carries the load, so tell parents to type the invoice number in the PayNow remark.
2. **Auto-confirm.** **Recommend** `exact_only` as the default (rules A and B only) with `never` available, and a per-centre trial month on `never` if they want. Alternative: always review in the first release.
3. **Retention and payer names.** **Recommend:** delete raw lines on resolution, unresolved after 60 days; keep fingerprints (hash) and name-free match records; keep **payer links as readable normalised names** (the centre already holds the parents' names; a person can see and forget them). Alternative: store only a hash (no list to view, no way to correct one by hand). Lawyer to confirm periods and the PDPA notice wording.
4. **Auto-matching a household's bulk payment (rule D).** **Recommend** suggestion only in v1. Alternative: auto when the payer is known and exactly one subset fits.
5. **Money fairness on small differences.** **Recommend** exact cents only, differences go to review. Alternative: a "within S$0.50" setting later if the first centre's bank nets fees (**not verified** that PayNow ever does).
6. **AI step.** **Recommend** leave out of the first release, decide after M5 once the review queue shows how many lines stay unmatched. Alternative: build it with M3 (adds a processor agreement before launch).
