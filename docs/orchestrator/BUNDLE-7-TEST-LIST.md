## What to expect and not to worry about
- **Tutor pay is not payment**: TutorDeck lists who is owed what and records what you paid by hand. Tutors see **only their own** pay and only as an estimate; never a family's fee, rate or invoice.
- **A tutor's request changes nothing until approved**; an approval re-checks every rule at that moment.
- **Statement matching never guesses money**; **import only creates**; **a make-up credit is earned once**.
