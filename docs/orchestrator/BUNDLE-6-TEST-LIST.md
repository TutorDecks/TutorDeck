# Bundle 6 — click-through for the owner (DEV preview only)

Only the **new** things since bundle 5: **centre import** (stage A), **tutor pay**, **tutors adding their own lessons and weekly slots**, **bank statement matching**, **emailing invoices to families**, **make-up credits** and the **data export**. Bundle 4's and 5's lists (`BUNDLE-4-TEST-LIST.md`, `BUNDLE-5-TEST-LIST.md`) still apply to everything older.

**Before you start**
- The DEV SQL pack for bundle 6 (`docs/orchestrator/dev-apply-pack.sql`, steps in `dev-apply-pack.md`) must be applied to the DEV project (bundles 4 and 5 must already be there), and the `dev` preview must have redeployed (page version **v356**). It is the **Test version · dummy data only** site.
- **Sample files** (all invented, nothing real) are in `docs/orchestrator/bundle-6-samples/`: `import-good/` (8 students, 3 tutors, 12 weekly lessons, no mistakes), `import-with-errors/` (the same kind of files with 3 mistakes put in on purpose; `planted.json` lists them) and `statement-sample-template.csv` (a made-up bank statement). Download them from the repository on GitHub (private repo: sign in) or ask for them to be sent.
- To make **more** of them: `node tests/fixtures/make-import-fixture.js --students 8 --tutors 3 --lessons 12 --errors 0 --out some/folder`, and `node tests/fixtures/make-statement-sample.js DLC-0003=54.50 DLC-0004=120 > statement.csv` (put your real DEV invoice numbers and exact amounts in).

## A. Switch the new options on (owner) — do this first
1. [ ] `centre.owner@` → More → **Centre settings** → step **4 How lessons are billed**: **Make-up credits** = *1 missed lesson = 1 credit*; **A credit is earned when a lesson is marked** = *Cancelled in time or late*; **Credits expire after** = 30 days; **The make-up lesson is billed** = *Never billed*. Leave **Billing starts from** empty (see the note at the end: the setting is saved and readable by the database, but the invoice run does **not** read it yet).
2. [ ] Step **5 Invoicing**: **Shareable invoice link** on; **Email invoices to parents** = *A person sends them (staff who issue invoices press Send)*; **Email a receipt when a payment is recorded** on; **Email payment reminders for overdue invoices** on (first after 3 days, repeat every 7, at most 3). Leave the wording boxes empty (our default wording is used).
3. [ ] Step **6 How parents pay**: tick **Bank statement matching**; **Bank for statement matching** = *Custom CSV columns* (DBS / OCBC / UOB are greyed "coming soon" on purpose); **Matched payments are** = *Marked paid automatically when the invoice number and the exact amount match…*.
4. [ ] Step **7 Tutors and lessons**: **Who edits the timetable** = *Tutors may change their own lessons (staff are told)*; underneath switch **on**: *A tutor may add a one-off lesson*, *A tutor may add, change or stop their own weekly slots*, *A tutor may create their own lessons from their weekly slots*. (*A tutor may set the online lesson link* is greyed "coming soon".)
5. [ ] Step **8 Tutor pay**: **Tutor pay** = *Full pay calculation*; **Pay tutors for a lesson** = *Only after the parent's payment is received and reconciled*; **Pay rate** = *Per lesson*; **Bonuses and deductions** on. *Tutors see* is greyed "coming soon" (tutors never see pay). *Paying tutors → Bulk payment file* is greyed too. Save / launch the settings.

## B. Import data (stage A) with the generated files — owner only
6. [ ] Owner → More → **Centre settings** → step **9 Import data** (the same page is the owner's Settings → Import). Six template buttons (families, students, tutors, weekly lessons, deposits, unpaid invoices) + *Past lessons* marked **Later**. Download one template: a header row and a commented example row.
7. [ ] Choose the four files from **`import-with-errors/`** (families, students, tutors, weekly lessons; you may add the other two). Expect **3 problems**, each with the **file, line, column, the value, what is wrong and how to fix it** (see `planted.json`: a bad date in `students.csv` line 5, an unknown family in `students.csv` line 2, a bad phone in `tutors.csv` line 3). **Copy errors** and **Download errors (CSV)** work. There is **no Import now** button while problems are listed. Nothing was written to the database.
8. [ ] Remove those files and choose the four files from **`import-good/`**. Expect no problems and a summary (8 students, 3 tutors, 12 weekly lessons, **0 emails sent**), a note to **download your data first**, and a **consent tick**. **Import now** stays off until you tick.
9. [ ] Tick and press **Import now** → one all-or-nothing import. Check the results: Students shows the 8 new students (with their families and guardians); More → **People** shows the **3 new tutors as invited** (no login was created, no email was sent); the timetable shows the weekly slots. (**Billing starts from** can be saved and read back, but nothing uses it yet: the invoice run still bills every lesson; that is a known gap, listed at the end.)
10. [ ] Choose the **same four files again** and import → **nothing is created twice** (everything is reported as already there / skipped). No duplicate students.
11. [ ] The **History** list shows the batch, when it can be undone until (7 days) and its status. Press **Undo** → a **preview** says what would be removed and what is kept (anything edited, marked or invoiced since is kept, with the reason) → confirm → the 8 students, the 3 tutor invitations and the weekly slots are gone; status **undone**. Pressing Undo again deletes nothing more.
12. [ ] Re-import `import-good/` once more if you want the students back for the next sections (the demo students are used in sections C–G anyway).

## C. Tutor pay (owner and staff only; tutors see nothing)
13. [ ] Owner → More → **Tutor pay** (a new page; also for `centre.staff@`, who has *Run tutor pay*). Three tabs: **Pay list · Rates · Lessons taught**. A full-pay centre opens on the Pay list; the page says tutors never see pay.
14. [ ] **Rates** → for **Demo Tutor One** set the **usual rate**: *per lesson*, S$50, from the 1st of this month, a reason ("Starting rate"). It appears in the history as *In force*. Add a second rate for **one student** (e.g. Aiden Tan, S$70, from the 1st): the student's rate beats the usual rate. Try a percentage above 100% → refused in words. Set a rate from a **future date** → shown as *Coming*, the old one stays in force until then.
15. [ ] **Pay list** (pick this month, or last month if that is where the demo lessons are): Demo Tutor One is listed with what is due, what is **held until families pay**, what **waits for a charge** and *no rate* for tutors without one. With the default rule nothing is payable until the family has paid, so most of it shows as **held**.
16. [ ] Release some: Money → **Invoice run** → create and approve the month's drafts → **Invoices & payments** → record the family's full payment on one invoice (as in bundle 4/5) → back on **Tutor pay** the lessons of that invoice are now **payable**; the rest stays held. Reverse that family payment → the lessons go back to held from that day.
17. [ ] Tap the tutor → the sheet lists each lesson with its state (*Payable / Held / Charge not typed*). **Record a payment** of part of what is due → *Part paid*; try more than is due → refused with the amount; put a whole account number in the reference → refused ("last 4 digits"). Record the rest → *Paid*. **Reverse** one payment → it stays listed as *Reversed* (nothing is edited).
18. [ ] **Add a bonus** with no reason → "Say why"; with a reason it appears as *Bonus · <reason>*. A deduction works the same way. After a period is recorded as paid, adding something dated inside it is refused.
19. [ ] **Download the list (CSV)** → payee, employment, amount, reference, period; **no bank details**; a tutor owed nothing is not on it. The download is written to the audit log first (nothing in it is bank data).
20. [ ] **Lessons taught** tab: counts and hours per tutor, cancelled and trial lessons, **no money anywhere**.
21. [ ] `tutor.one@` / `tutor.two@`: **no Tutor pay** in their menu; typing `/centre-tutor-pay` sends them to My lessons. `centre.frontdesk@`: no Tutor pay either. A staff member without *Run tutor pay* (switch it off for `centre.staff@` in People → permissions, then back on) has no entry and the address says "not available".

## D. Tutors add their own one-off lessons and weekly slots (needs section A step 4)
22. [ ] `tutor.one@` → **Schedule** → **Add a lesson**: pick one of *your assigned students*, a day more than 24 h ahead, times, subject, a note → saved. No price field anywhere. A trial or a group class cannot be added.
23. [ ] Same screen → **My weekly slots** → tap one → change the time, or stop it from a day; **Add a weekly slot** (student, weekday, times). Each add / change / stop counts as one change against the monthly limit and the notice rule from section A.
24. [ ] **Create lessons** → pick a last day (at most 62 days ahead) → lessons appear for *your own* slots only. Press it again → nothing is made twice. Holidays and ended students are skipped.
25. [ ] `centre.staff@` (or the owner) → Home → **Tutor changes** (the amber flag): the added lesson, the slot change and one line for the *Create lessons* press are listed. Staff pressing *Create lessons* on the timetable afterwards makes nothing twice either.
26. [ ] **Price rule:** a lesson or slot the tutor made for a student who already has a **staff-made** slot or lesson copies that price. 27. [ ] Switch **Who edits the timetable** back to *Admins only* → the tutor's *Add a lesson / My weekly slots / Create lessons* disappear after a reload.

## E. Bank statement matching (owner or staff with *Record payments*; section A step 3)
28. [ ] Make sure there are **two issued, unpaid invoices** (Money → Invoice run → create and approve if needed). Note each invoice's **number** (e.g. `DLC-0007`) and **exact amount**. Make the sample file: edit `statement-sample-template.csv` (replace `DLC-0001` / `DLC-0002` and their amounts `100.00` / `54.50` with yours), **or** run `node tests/fixtures/make-statement-sample.js DLC-0007=54.50 DLC-0008=120 > statement.csv`.
29. [ ] Owner → **Money** → **Statements** tab (the sixth tab, after Late fees). It says the file is read on your device and lines are deleted once settled. **Upload a statement** → choose the CSV. The first lines are the bank's own header text, so **map the columns**: *header rows* 4, *date* = Date (day/month/year), *amount* = separate Withdrawal and Deposit columns, *reference* = Description, *payer* = Payer, *bank reference* = Reference. The **preview** shows the first lines and totals (money in / money out); a wrong choice shows "N rows don't read correctly" with the row and reason, and the Import button stays off until it is fixed.
30. [ ] **Import n lines.** Expect: the two lines whose reference holds an invoice number and the **exact** amount are **paid automatically**; the "school fees S$60.00" line and the "S$77.77" line **need a decision**; the money-out line (supplier) is listed separately and **never matched**. Only the mapped columns were uploaded (the bank header lines and the account number never leave the device).
31. [ ] Open the waiting S$60.00 line → the sheet suggests an invoice **only if** one is owed for exactly that amount ("The amount matches exactly"); tick **Remember this payer** (on by default) → **Confirm** → a payment is recorded (method *bank*, the bank's own reference, **no payer name stored on the payment**) and the invoice is paid / part-paid. A line with an amount nobody owes stays waiting with the amount shown (exact cents only).
32. [ ] **Matched** lists the automatic and the confirmed ones; **Remembered payers** lists the payer name you ticked and which family it pays for (you can forget it). Upload **the same file again** → "N of these lines were uploaded before and will be skipped"; importing it adds nothing.
33. [ ] **Undo** one automatic match without typing a reason → stopped on the page; with a reason → the payment is **reversed** (a reversal entry, nothing edited) and a date-and-amount line comes back to the waiting list. **Not ours** sets a line aside; undo puts it back.
34. [ ] Switch **Matched payments are** to *Never automatic* → upload a fresh statement → every line waits for a person.

## What to expect and not to worry about
- **Tutor pay is not payment**: TutorDeck lists who is owed what and records what you paid by hand; it never moves money. Held pay rolls into the period the family pays in.
- **Statement matching never guesses money**: only an exact invoice number + exact amount, or a remembered payer paying exactly one invoice's exact amount, is automatic; everything else waits. **DBS / OCBC / UOB layouts are not built** until each is checked against a real file; custom columns work for any bank.
- **Import creates only**: it never edits or deletes anything that exists, never sends an email and never creates a login. Past lessons, deposits, unpaid invoices and pay rates are not imported yet (the templates for deposits and unpaid invoices are shown but are "stage B").
- **Make-up credits**: one missed lesson = one credit; a make-up is never charged twice. The per-period cap on make-ups and parent notices are *coming soon*.

## Needs a Brevo key / not testable on DEV
- **Actually delivering email** (section F steps 38–40) needs, in Vercel → **Preview** environment: `BREVO_API_KEY` (the DEV key), `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY` (the **DEV** project's), `INVITE_TEST_ALLOW` (your allow-listed test addresses), and the **CRON_SECRET** for the daily job. Without them the app still works: *Email invoice* queues the message and the page says it is **safe in the queue**; nothing is sent.
- **Bounces** (*Addresses that bounced*, the bounce flag on a guardian) need the Brevo webhook (`/api/brevo-webhook?s=<BREVO_WEBHOOK_SECRET>`) set up in Brevo and `BREVO_WEBHOOK_SECRET` in Vercel; DEV has no way to bounce a mail, so the screen only shows the empty state. The **daily email job** (`/api/email-cron`, 01:00 UTC) and **make-up expiry job** (`/api/credits-cron`, **not scheduled yet**) only run in production.
- **Google sign-in, Stripe** and everything about **real bank files** (DBS / OCBC / UOB) are not testable on DEV.
- **Known gaps, deliberately not built yet:** the invoice run **ignoring lessons before "Billing starts from"** (the setting and `org_billing_start()` exist; `centre_invoice_run_rows` does not read it yet); a staff screen to type the price of a tutor-made lesson for a student with no staff-made lesson (section D step 26); the **bank-file payout** for tutor pay (waits for your bank's answer); tutors seeing their own pay (your rule is no); **import stage B** (deposits and unpaid invoices) and past lessons; **closing a centre** (designed in `T35-closure-design.md`, not built); a per-period cap on make-ups.
- Anything that looks wrong: note the step number, the login, and a screenshot.
