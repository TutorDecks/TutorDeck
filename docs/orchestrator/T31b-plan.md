# T31b plan: TutorDeck invoices its centres, centres pay by PayNow

Branch `claude/t31b-plan`. Plan only: no product code, no SQL, nothing deployed, LIVE untouched. Written 2026-11-26. Read: CLAUDE.md, `db/migration_org_foundation.sql` (`platform_admins`, `org_plans`, `platform_fee_snapshots`, `audit_*`, `platform_orgs()`), `migration_billing.sql`, `migration_attendance.sql`, `api/billing-cron.js`, `assets/js/paynow.js`, `money.js`, `org.js` (Plan tab), `docs/business/SERVICE-AGREEMENT-FACTS.md`, T4 plan (branch `claude/t4-plan`). Anything I could not confirm says **not verified**. Tax and accounting points are for the owner's accountant; this is not advice.

## Summary
- **What exists:** each month the job writes an immutable **fee snapshot** per centre (`platform_fee_snapshots`: active and billable students, base, per-student rate, `fee_cents`). A `platform_admins` table and a read-only `platform_orgs()` function exist. **No screen, no invoice, no payment record and no platform admin login on DEV exist** (the demo seed has none).
- **What we build:** a small set of `td_*` tables plus `SECURITY DEFINER` functions: snapshot, then a **draft invoice**, then a platform admin **issues** it (number `TD-2026-11-0001`), the centre owner sees it in the **Plan tab** with a **PayNow QR** and the invoice number as reference, a platform admin **records the payment** by hand. TutorDeck never touches the money.
- **Reuse, not rewrite:** QR comes from `PayNow.build` (it already supports `type:"uen"` and a reference up to 25 characters, tag 62) and `qrlib.js`; money maths from `money.js` (integer cents, `gstForInvoice` exists); numbering, immutability and credit notes follow the T4 patterns, with TutorDeck as issuer.
- **Separate from the parent-invoice tables** (T4 `centre_invoices`): different issuer, customer and tax position. Solo flows and Stripe untouched.
- **Minimum viable version:** the first 1 to 3 centres can be invoiced from a spreadsheet and a PayNow screenshot. The app version starts paying off from about centre 3 or 4. Steps P0 to P4 below are the real MVP (about five small PRs).
- **Fee mismatch to settle first:** the owner said S$0.50 per billable student; the code, migrations and docs still say **S$0.40** (`org_plans` seed rows at foundation l.387 and l.424, `money.js` tests, CLAUDE.md, Service Agreement Facts). Who changes it is **not verified** (T31a?). The invoice reads the rate from the snapshot, so nothing here hard-codes either number.
- Six decisions for the owner in section 9.

## 0. Scope update (orchestrator, 2026-11-26): two payment methods per centre
A centre may pay TutorDeck by **Stripe (self-serve, if it sets it up itself)** or by **invoice + PayNow (managed)**. This plan is the managed option. Both read the same fee snapshot, so the amount is identical either way.
- **Switch flag:** `org_plans.billing_method` = `'stripe'` or `'invoice'` (effective-dated like the other plan columns; column and its default are **not built, not verified**; T31a owns the Stripe side). Only a **platform admin** sets `invoice`, when a centre contacts us. Default for a new centre: `stripe`.
- **What each side sees.** *stripe:* Plan tab shows the Stripe status and the portal button, plus a line "Prefer an invoice and PayNow? Contact us, we set it up with you" (mailto/WhatsApp link; no form). *invoice:* Plan tab shows TutorDeck invoices (section 4) and **no** Stripe buttons. The admin page shows the method per centre.
- **Stripe to invoice (mid-year):** (1) admin picks an effective month, never the current month once its snapshot or Stripe renewal exists; (2) the centre's Stripe subscription is **cancelled at period end by hand** (so the last Stripe-billed month is paid and not refunded; mid-period proration is avoided), then `stripe_subscription_id` is kept for history; (3) `billing_method` becomes `invoice` from the first month after; (4) the draft function refuses a month the Stripe subscription still covers (no double billing) and the job's Stripe update skips `invoice` centres; (5) an unpaid or failed Stripe invoice stays a Stripe matter: the admin settles it in Stripe first, or records it as an opening balance on the first TutorDeck invoice (accountant to confirm).
- **Invoice to Stripe:** all issued TutorDeck invoices stay as they are; unpaid ones remain payable by PayNow until paid or credited; the centre starts a subscription itself from the Plan tab and the first Stripe month is the first month with no TutorDeck invoice. A month is never billed on both.
- **Fee snapshots:** written for every centre in every case (append-only, never changed by a switch). A test: one centre-month has either a TutorDeck invoice or a Stripe charge, never both.
- **MVP for the first managed customers (Opus):** P0 to P4 only, and the very first invoices may still be a spreadsheet (section 8). Not built until needed: P5 onward, and the self-serve switch screen (the admin changes the flag by one function call, `platform_set_billing_method(org, method, from_month)`, audited).

## 1. Monthly cycle
1. **Snapshot** (already built, 00:30 SGT on the 1st, production only): counts last month's active students, rounds to the nearest 10 (halves up), writes `fee_cents = base + rate x billable`. Append-only. Billing is **in advance**: the fee for month M uses month M-1's count.
2. **Draft invoice per centre** (new): created by a platform admin pressing "Create drafts for 2026-11", or automatically by the same job later. One draft per centre and month (unique on `org_id, month`), and it **refuses** a centre whose `billing_method` is `stripe` for that month (no double billing). Lines are copied from the snapshot, not recomputed:
   | Line | Amount |
   |---|---|
   | Platform fee, base, month M | `base_cents` |
   | Billable students: N x rate (active count and rounding shown in the note) | `billable x per_student_cents` |
   | Adjustment or discount (optional, signed, **reason required**, draft only) | typed cents |
   A test asserts that unadjusted lines add up to `fee_cents` of the snapshot.
3. **Review and issue** by a platform admin. Issue assigns the number, sets invoice date, **due date = invoice date + N days** (platform setting, suggest 14), and freezes the bill-to details (centre name, address, UEN, billing email as they are that day; where these live today is **not verified**, so P0 adds a "billing details" block the owner fills in).
4. **GST: none today** (TutorDeck is not GST-registered; the document says "Invoice", not "Tax invoice", and carries no GST line). If TutorDeck registers: a platform setting `gst_registered_from` (date) and rate in basis points. Invoices issued **on or after** that date become "Tax Invoice" with GST number, computed **once per invoice, half up** by the existing `gstForInvoice`. Already issued invoices never change. Whether the S$100 / S$0.50 prices become GST-exclusive or inclusive is a pricing decision then (accountant).
5. **Delivery:** now, a **secure link** (random token, a public function that returns only that invoice, like the solo `invoice_by_token`) and a **copyable message** (WhatsApp/email text with link, amount, due date, reference). The centre owner also sees it in the Plan tab. Later, email via the T7 Brevo setup (sender `noreply@tutordeck.com.sg`, reply-to `tutordeckadmin@gmail.com`; a real support mailbox is still a prerequisite for the first paying centre per the board).

## 2. TutorDeck's PayNow
- **Platform settings, not secrets and not in code:** an append-only, effective-dated `platform_settings` row (like `org_settings`): legal name, UEN, PayNow type and ID, bank name and account for transfers, invoice due days, grace days, GST fields, invoice footer text. Edited only by a platform admin through a function; changes audited. The PayNow UEN is public on every invoice, so it is not a secret, but it must not live in the repo (a test greps for it).
- **QR:** the browser builds it with `PayNow.build({type:"uen", id, amount: balance_due/100, name:"TutorDeck", reference:"TD-2026-11-0001"})`. Fixed amount. For a part-paid invoice the QR shows the **remaining balance**. Reference = invoice number (15 characters, within the 25 limit). **Bank transfer** (name, bank, account, same reference) is printed beside it as the alternative. Whether bank apps accept the reference tag from this builder is **not verified**: one real S$0.01-style test payment from the owner's phone is part of P3 "done".
- Stored: the QR is never stored; it is drawn from the stored amount and settings.

## 3. Payments, statuses, non-payment
- **Stored status** only `draft`, `issued`, `void`. **Paid, part-paid and overdue are derived** from payments and the due date, so they can never disagree with the records.
- **Record payment** (platform admin): date received, amount, method (PayNow, bank transfer, other), bank reference or note, which invoice. Always an append-only row; a mistake is a **reversal row** with a reason, never an edit. **Part payments** allowed (balance shown). **Overpayment** is held as the centre's **credit balance** (a ledger row); an admin applies it to a later invoice with an explicit action (no silent automatic use). A **refund** of credit is recorded as a row too (TutorDeck pays it by hand).
- **Void:** an issued invoice is never edited or deleted. A full **credit note** (`TDC-2026-11-0001`, reason, references the invoice) sets it to `void`; a partial credit note reduces the balance. If payment was already received, the credit becomes credit balance or a recorded refund.
- **Reminders:** manual now. The admin screen lists overdue invoices with a "copy reminder" text (first at due date + 1 day, second at +14); each send is logged (who, when) by a button "I sent this". Email reminders later via T7.
- **If a centre does not pay** (settings, owner decides defaults): `grace_days` after due date (suggest 14). From due date the centre owner sees a **warning banner in the Plan tab** (and a small notice for the owner on Home, **not verified where is least intrusive**). After grace: banner turns urgent. **Optional read-only mode** after a second period (suggest off by default, decision 4): a function `org_billing_state(org)` returns `ok` / `overdue` / `restricted`; restricted blocks **new** business writes for centres only through the existing write path (**not verified how invasive: needs its own design step**), never blocks reading, exporting, **tutors marking attendance** (decision 4) or the owner paying. **Data is never deleted** because of non-payment; closing an account follows the Terms and the data-export rules.

## 4. The centre's view (owner only)
Plan tab (`tabPlan` in `assets/js/org.js`, which today shows the plan and the snapshots): add a list of TutorDeck invoices (number, month, amount, status chip, due date); the invoice page shows lines, bill-to, status, **QR + reference + bank details**, a **Download PDF** button (the browser print-to-PDF page, as solo invoices do; **not verified** that a server-made PDF is needed), and the history of payments received. Read through `centre_my_td_invoices(org)` returning only that centre's issued, void or paid rows, **never drafts**. Staff and tutors see nothing (owner-only, like the plan today).

## 5. Platform admin screens
- **Who:** a row in `platform_admins` (two people: the founders). Reasons to keep it that simple: two admins, every write function records `auth.uid()` in the audit log. Admins are added **only** by SQL on each project (never by a browser), and a platform admin still cannot read student records without a centre's time-limited support grant (existing rule).
- **Where:** a new page `platform.html` (route `/platform`), reachable by direct URL; its menu entry appears only if `platform_whoami()` says yes. New files, so little conflict with other workers.
- **Screens (one page, three sections):** (a) **Centres this month:** one row per centre from `platform_orgs()` plus snapshot, draft or invoice status, balance; (b) **Invoice detail:** lines, adjustment, **Issue**, **Record payment**, **Reverse payment**, **Credit note / Void**, copy link, copy message; (c) **Settings and export:** payee settings, **CSV export** of invoices, payments and credit notes for a date range (number, date, centre, net, GST, total, paid, method, reference: columns agreed with the accountant, **not verified**). One primary action per screen, house style.
- **Safety:** every write is a function; the screen shows a confirm line ("Issue TD-2026-11-0001 to Opus Tuition for S$X") before issue and payment.

## 6. Data model (all new tables: RLS on, **no insert/update/delete policy for browsers**)
| Table | Notes |
|---|---|
| `platform_settings` | append-only, effective-dated (as `org_settings`); read by platform admins; payee fields via `td_pay_details()` for invoice viewers |
| `td_invoices` | org, month, status draft/issued/void, number (null until issue), invoice and due dates, bill-to snapshot, GST fields frozen, totals in cents, share token, `snapshot_month`; unique (org, month) |
| `td_invoice_lines` | invoice, kind (base, students, adjustment), description, cents; lines of an issued invoice cannot change (trigger) |
| `td_payments` | org, invoice (nullable for credit), amount, date, method, reference, `reverses` (nullable), recorded_by; append-only |
| `td_credit_notes` | number `TDC-...`, invoice, amount, reason; append-only |
| `td_counters` | one row per (kind, year, month), locked `for update` inside the issue function, so numbers have **no gaps**; the number is taken at **issue**, never at draft |
| `td_reminders` | invoice, sent_by, at, note |
- **Immutability:** `audit_immutable` triggers on payments, credit notes, counters (no delete), settings, reminders; invoice rows update only through the issue and void functions; issued lines locked. `audit_row` on all tables (as the other tables).
- **Functions** (`SECURITY DEFINER`, pinned `search_path`, `org_is_platform_admin()` or owner check first, `anon` revoked): `platform_td_create_drafts(month)`, `platform_td_add_adjustment`, `platform_td_issue`, `platform_td_record_payment`, `platform_td_reverse_payment`, `platform_td_credit_note`, `platform_td_log_reminder`, `platform_td_export(from,to)`, `platform_td_save_settings`, `platform_whoami()`, `centre_my_td_invoices(org)`, `td_invoice_by_token(t)`, `td_pay_details()`.
- **Gotcha to decide (decision 3):** the foundation says new tables with `org_id` need `on delete cascade` so account deletion works. For **invoices that is wrong**: tax records may have to be kept after a centre leaves. Recommend **no cascade on these tables** (plain `org_id` with the bill-to snapshot stored on the invoice) and an explicit rule in the centre-deletion path; how `org_delete` treats centres today is **not verified**.
- **Tests (`tests/sql/td_billing_test.sql`, run by `bash tests/sql/run.sh`, plus a `money`-style JS test for line totals):** one test per rule: numbering has no gaps and survives rollback; a centre owner reads only its own issued invoices and no drafts; a different centre, staff, tutor, solo and anon read nothing; non-admin calls are refused; issued rows reject update and delete; payment reversal restores the balance; overpayment becomes credit; part payment status; derived overdue at the due date edge and one day after; GST off gives no GST line, GST on gives once, half up; centre with `billing_method = stripe` refused; switch-month edge cases.

## 8. First customer path (Opus Tuition) and what stays manual
**Path:** they contact us, then a service agreement (owner's lawyer, facts in `docs/business/SERVICE-AGREEMENT-FACTS.md`), then the centre is created on the platform (**who creates it and how is not verified**: self sign-up wizard vs by hand), then their plan row in `org_plans` (S$100 base, S$0.50 per student, agreed start date) and their billing details; then on the 1st of each month: snapshot, draft, issue, payment.
**Manual at the very start (recommended for centres 1 to 3):** the owner copies the month's snapshot numbers from the Plan tab, makes the invoice in a spreadsheet with the same number format `TD-YYYY-MM-NNNN`, sends it with a PayNow screenshot, ticks "paid" in the sheet, and keeps the CSV for the accountant. Nothing about this blocks the later migration (the app starts at the next number: P2 supports setting the first counter). The **minimum viable app** is P0 to P4; P5 to P6 can follow when a centre actually pays late.
Also before the first paying centre (already on the board): a real support mailbox, Terms updated (they say the service is free, s.5), live Stripe untouched for centres.

## 9. Risks and decisions for the owner (recommendation first)
1. **Rate S$0.40 or S$0.50?** Recommend S$0.50 as agreed, via a new effective-dated `org_plans` row per centre (not a code edit), and update the docs and tests in one PR owned by T31a or the orchestrator. Alternative: keep 0.40 for Opus as a launch price.
2. **Manual first or build first?** Recommend a spreadsheet for the first 1 to 3 centres, build P0 to P4 meanwhile. Alternative: build before invoicing anyone (safer records, slower start).
3. **Keeping invoices after a centre leaves.** Recommend no cascade delete on `td_*` tables and a bill-to snapshot on each invoice. Alternative: cascade like other tables (simpler, but tax records vanish). Accountant to confirm the retention period (often said to be 5 years: **not verified**).
4. **Non-payment policy.** Recommend: banner after the due date, manual reminders, 14 grace days, **no** automatic lock-out for v1 (P8 optional, tutors always able to mark attendance). Alternative: read-only after 30 days overdue. Never delete data.
5. **Due days and billing in advance.** Recommend 14 days from invoice date, invoice on the 1st for that month (fee uses last month's count, as built). Alternative: bill in arrears on the last day of the month. Accountant to confirm how an invoice dated before the service month ends is treated.
6. **Accounting points to confirm with the accountant (not advice):** invoice wording while not GST-registered (and the registration threshold, often said to be S$1m taxable turnover: **not verified**); whether the number series can restart each month; how part payments, credit balances and refunds should be recorded; the CSV columns; treatment of unpaid invoices (bad debt); whether a payment received before issue is allowed; and whether TutorDeck's UEN and bank details printed on invoices need any wording.
